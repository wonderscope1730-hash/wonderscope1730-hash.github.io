Self Improving Writing Agent

Self Improving Writing Agent is a privately operated research and creative-writing automation pilot. It helps its operator prepare, evaluate, and preserve versioned fiction drafts and supporting evidence.

The app connects to Google Drive only after the operator grants access. It uses the limited drive.file permission to work with app-created or explicitly selected files, primarily inside the designated writing-project folder. It is not a public writing service and does not offer general user registration.

Project data is managed in private Google Drive, GitHub, and hosted PostgreSQL systems. Access to third-party model providers and any publication of fiction are separate controlled actions, not consequences of visiting this page.

Read the privacy policy to understand how this application handles Google account and file data.
